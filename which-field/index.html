Which Field?

Topic 5 · Data Buses — ARINC 429 Word Decoding

Nine real decode/encode jobs on a 32-bit ARINC 429 word. For each one: identify the field responsible (Label, SDI, Data Field, SSM or Parity), then the specific value it carries. Every verdict traces to a stated fact or worked example.

01

Predict, then check

8 minutes · no AI
  1. Screens down. On paper, for cases 1, 2, 4 and 9 below: name the field and its value, with one sentence why.
  2. Then open the tool and check all four. Mark each right or wrong.
The wrong ones are the useful ones — write one line on what fact you were missing, not just "I was wrong".
02

Make AI commit

10 minutes · AI never gives the answer first

Paste this, exactly as written, about Case 8 (the tricky SSM case):

I am an aerospace electronics student.
Here is an ARINC 429 word situation: [paste
Case 8's brief]. Make FOUR specific,
testable claims: which field the bits
belong to, what the bit pattern means, and
two supporting facts about why that meaning
applies to THIS word. Number them 1 to 4.
Do not hedge.
Open Case 8 in the tool and adjudicate every claim against it. Mark each true or false, and for each false one, say what AI got wrong.
03

Settle it

9 minutes · together, then alone
  • Scores on the board. How many of four did AI get right on Case 8?
  • One question for the room: bits 30-31 = 11 on a BCD word and bits 30-31 = 11 on a BNR word — why is the meaning not the same?
  • Transfer (no tool, justify from the comparison table below): your FMC must broadcast the same present-position word to 6 other systems that never need to talk back to it. Which standard, ARINC 429 or ARINC 629 — and why?
Finish these two, in writing:
• Before today I thought the SSM field always meant the same thing, because …
• AI got __ of 4 right on Case 8. The one it got wrong was wrong because …
