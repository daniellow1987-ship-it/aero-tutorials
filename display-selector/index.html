Display Technology Selector

Topic 9 · Electronic Displays

Eight real avionics display jobs. For each one: pick the technology family (CRT / LCD / LED), then the sub-type the deck actually distinguishes within it (scanning method, illumination mode, matrix type, or display format). Every verdict is drawn straight from the topic's own stated facts.

01

Predict, then check

8 minutes · no AI
  1. Screens down. On paper, for cases A, C, E and G below: name the technology family and the sub-type, and write one sentence why.
  2. Then open the selector and check all four. Mark each right or wrong.
The wrong ones are the useful ones — write one line on what fact you were missing, not just "I was wrong".
02

Make AI commit

10 minutes · AI never gives the answer first

Paste this, exactly as written, about Case B (the HUD symbology case):

I am an aerospace electronics student.
Here is a display requirement: [paste
Case B's brief]. Make FIVE specific,
testable claims: the technology family,
the exact sub-type, and three supporting
facts about why that sub-type fits. Number
them 1 to 5. Do not hedge.
Open Case B in the selector and adjudicate every claim against it. Mark each true or false, and for each false one, say what AI got wrong.
03

Settle it

9 minutes · together, then alone
  • Scores on the board. How many of five did AI get right on Case B?
  • One question for the room: why doesn't "LED display" just mean an LED backlight behind an LCD panel?
  • Transfer (no selector, justify from the spec matrix below): a light trainer aircraft needs a simple numeric heading readout, tight budget, no power budget for a backlight, must be legible in full daylight in an unshaded cockpit, doesn't need to work at night. Family, sub-type, and why?
Finish these two, in writing:
• Before today I thought "LCD" and "LED display" were basically the same thing because …
• AI got __ of 5 right on Case B. The one it got wrong was wrong because …
